# Session recovery contract

**Coverage** FR-007–008 and SC-003.

- The physical step owns stream, tool and assistant settlement. The logical runner owns model choice, context reload, retry wait, and dispatch. It carries a distinct-only recovery outcome separately from accepted ordinary retry. With no eligible fallback, the new outcome propagates the original terminal failure using the same error identity.
- Cancellation and unsafe interruption retain their settlement and handoff behavior. Context overflow retains compaction precedence and never causes model failover for that decision. `ContentPolicy`, provider content filtering and tool declines or blocked work bar fallback. Executing calls with unknown effects bar dispatch regardless of synthetic cleanup. Keep ordinary provider failure behavior for unsupported unsafe cases.
- A classifier-confirmed 401 Authentication failure, with no override and no output or tools, sends one primary and one eligible fallback, with zero primary retries. Exhaustion of the current retry schedule or hard timeout budget can dispatch a distinct fallback without another primary. A normal retry still invokes the session retry hook where its budget yields a decision, uses normalized delay and existing retry events, and tries fallback after backoff as before. Hook decisions control the ordinary retry, including their in-budget override ability. They cannot replenish a hard budget or disable the independently safe distinct-only branch.
- For safe continuation, keep the settled tool calls and results in existing session history. Load the history for the effective fallback model and start a new assistant. Do not re-execute old calls or treat a cleanup-generated error as evidence of a known effect. Transparent restarts require no unsafe visible work. Existing partial-output ordinary recovery continues under its current history rules.
- Resolve staged, committed and legacy precedence through the existing chooser. Explicit off, unavailable target, an effective same-model selection, and an already attempted identity leave no fallback. Validate the newest intent at the dispatch boundary, including across async preparation and compaction. Variant is part of request attribution and excluded from duplicate identity. The fallback is an attempt-local selection. It never updates the session's selected primary.
